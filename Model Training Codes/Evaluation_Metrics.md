We have shared the Google Drive links for both the **ConvNeXt** and **EfficientNet** models. Each folder contains the corresponding evaluation metrics recorded during our experiments, along with the trained model weights (`.pth` files).

- **ConvNeXt**: The trained model weights are available as `best_convnext_tiny.pth`.
- **EfficientNet**: The trained model weights are available as `best_effientnet_bo.pth`.

### Google Drive Links:

- **ConvNeXt**: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1XCThfS8nvflbRdaHH9jo1btaOMwt1gnX?usp=sharing
- **EfficientNet**: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1RyH-aMcX_pywavy8rCXObVM4zlNOeZrA?usp=sharing

Based on the evaluation results, we selected **ConvNeXt** as our final model due to its superior performance compared to EfficientNet, which is also reflected in the recorded evaluation metrics.

Please note that the evaluation metrics for the **ResNet** model are currently unavailable; therefore, we are unable to provide them at this time.
